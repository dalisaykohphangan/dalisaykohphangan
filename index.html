About Dalisay

A quiet place
that belongs
to the land.

Tucked away in a lush tropical setting, Dalisay is an adults-only, family-run, eco-conscious resort designed for peaceful getaways and creative stays.

Inspired by traditional Thai architecture with modern comforts, each bungalow offers privacy, serenity, and a deep connection to nature.
