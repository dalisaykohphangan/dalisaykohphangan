Where we eat.

Places we love, starting with those a few minutes from Dalisay.

01

Close to Dalisay

Our suggestions around Hin Kong, a short walk or ride away.

  • Tomorrow Coconut Lane

    A new spot very close to Dalisay for coffee, breakfast and lunch!

    Map ↗

  • Pura Vida Cafe

    Breakfast, brunch and coffee, with vegetarian and vegan options. There's a second cafe at Haad Yao beach.

    Hin Kong ↗Haad Yao ↗

  • Indigo Specialty Coffee & Bakery

    A lovely spot for coffee, breakfast, brunch and freshly baked treats.

    Map ↗

  • Deli Devi

    A plant-based cafe and deli for breakfast, brunch, vegan cheeses, chocolate and cacao drinks.

    Map ↗

  • Pure Vegan Heaven

    Plant-based food for breakfast or lunch.

    Map ↗

  • Kikekla Bar & Restaurant

    Beachfront food and cocktails. A lovely spot for a meal or sunset drinks.

    Map ↗

  • Romanzo Tropicale

    Italian food by the beach. A lovely option for dinner around sunset.

    Map ↗

  • Happiness Restaurant

    Beachfront Thai food and seafood with views across Hin Kong Bay.

    Map ↗

  • L'Alcove

    A beachfront bistro for dinner, wine and sunset drinks.

    Map ↗

  • Cintamani

    A cocktail bar and tapas restaurant with delightful decor and sunset views.

    Map ↗

  • Mana Beach Bar Cafe

    A beachside stop for drinks and a relaxed afternoon.

    Map ↗

  • El Barrio

    Mexican food and cocktails. A fun choice for an evening out.

    Map ↗

  • Dar Mansour

    Moroccan dining with slow-cooked tagines and a candlelit garden setting. Reserve ahead and ask about pre-ordering.

    Map ↗

  • Sati Pot

    Persian food with fragrant rice dishes and rich flavours.

    Map ↗

02

Thai food

From MICHELIN-recommended dining to simple local favourites.

  • Dear Phangan

    Wok Tum

    MICHELIN Guide recommended Thai dining with a seasonal menu featuring local ingredients. Reservations are essential.

    Map ↗

  • Nong Taa Ped Palo

    Wok Tum

    Super simple, cheap Thai food. A great option for a casual local meal!

    Map ↗

  • Whum Noi Noi

    Nai Wok

    A chill spot on the beach serving authentic Thai food. Recommended for a relaxed evening meal and drinks.

    Map ↗

  • The Wave Sunset Bar

    Thai food on a private beach and a great sunset spot. Lovely for chilling or working during the day.

    Map ↗

  • Baan Boontom Somtum & Gallery

    Thong Sala

    Thai and Isaan food in a charming wooden house with an art gallery atmosphere.

    Map ↗

  • Nong View

    Baan Tai

    Local Thai food with Southern Thai flavours.

    Map ↗

  • Krua Chao Koh

    Baan Tai

    Southern Thai food and seafood. A lovely choice for exploring local flavours.

    Map ↗

  • Madam Saak Thong

    Chaloklum

    A family-run restaurant serving Thai and Isaan dishes.

    Map ↗

  • Chicken Rice Shop

    Thong Sala

    Simple, affordable Thai comfort food. Stop for a plate of chicken rice or pork with rice.

    Map ↗

  • Pork Leg Restaurant

    Thong Sala

    A simple local spot for braised pork leg and Thai noodle soup.

    Map ↗

03

Night markets & street food

Two weekly markets worth planning your evening around.

  • Saturday Night Market

    Thong Sala

    Explore the walking street on Saturday evening for street food, sweet treats, shopping and a lively atmosphere.

    Map ↗

  • Sunday Night Market

    Chaloklum

    Visit the fishing village on Sunday evening for food stalls, desserts and a lovely atmosphere by the waterfront.

    Map ↗
