About us

Tucked away in
a lush tropical
setting.

Dalisay is an adults-only, family-run, eco-conscious resort designed for peaceful getaways and creative stays.

Inspired by traditional Thai architecture with modern comforts, each bungalow offers privacy, serenity, and a deep connection to nature. Raised on stilts with deep verandas and generous glass doors, they open to the garden and let the breeze move through.
